# CodeAtlas v3.2 Enterprise Expansion Architecture Specification

## Executive Overview

CodeAtlas v3.2 transforms CodeAtlas into an enterprise-grade engineering intelligence, governance, and autonomy platform. It supports complex multi-unit corporate hierarchies, automated SSO/SCIM directory synchronization, real-time SIEM audit stream forwarding, executive CTO dashboards, engineering Policy as Code (Rego/OPA), searchable Enterprise Service Catalogs, and organizational ROI metrics.

---

## 6-Level Enterprise Organizational Hierarchy

```
Organization (Acme Global)
    └── Business Unit (Core Infrastructure)
            └── Department (Cloud Platform)
                    └── Team (Identity & Security)
                            └── Workspace (Auth Services Workspace)
                                    └── Repository (auth_service_repo)
                                            └── Microservice (auth_service)
```

- **Inherited Governance**: Permissions and ABAC policies inherit safely downwards with strict isolation boundaries.

---

## Identity, SSO, SCIM & SIEM Audit Streaming

- **SSO**: SAML 2.0 and OIDC support with domain verification and mandatory SSO enforcement.
- **SCIM 2.0**: Automated user provisioning, deprovisioning, group synchronization, and role binding.
- **SIEM Event Streaming**: Real-time audit event forwarding to Splunk, Datadog SIEM, Elastic, and Microsoft Sentinel.
