# Engineering Strategy & Optimization Architecture

## 1. Vision & Purpose

CodeAtlas Engineering Strategy converts engineering state and risk evidence into strategic decision support:

$$ENGINEERING\ STATE \rightarrow RISKS \rightarrow OPPORTUNITIES \rightarrow OPTIONS \rightarrow SIMULATION \rightarrow PRIORITIZATION \rightarrow INVESTMENT \rightarrow OUTCOME$$

---

## 2. Core Pillars

1. **Strategic Objectives & Constraints**: Organization-defined goals (e.g. `REDUCE_ARCHITECTURE_RISK`, `IMPROVE_RELIABILITY`) under explicit capacity constraints.
2. **Option Generation & Trade-off Engine**: Evaluates candidate options (Option A, B, C, D) with explicit risk deltas and effort levels.
3. **Multi-Scenario Simulator**: Reuses v1.2 Simulation Studio Engine for virtual graph scenario comparisons ($Scenario\ A\ vs\ B$).
4. **Roadmap Sequence**: Sequences initiatives into relative phases ($NOW \rightarrow NEXT \rightarrow LATER \rightarrow OPTIONAL$).
5. **Organizational AI Strategist**: Evidence-grounded RAG assistant with guardrails.
