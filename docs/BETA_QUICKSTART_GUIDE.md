# CodeAtlas Beta Developer Quickstart & Onboarding Guide

Welcome to the CodeAtlas Private Beta! CodeAtlas is an engineering intelligence platform that helps developers, software architects, engineering managers, and CTOs understand, investigate, simulate, and improve complex multi-repository software systems.

---

## Your First 10 Minutes with CodeAtlas

Follow this continuous developer journey to achieve your first value moment:

```
Step 1: Connect Repository (/repositories)
       ↓
Step 2: Explore Architecture & Knowledge Graph (/architecture & /knowledge)
       ↓
Step 3: Investigate Risks & Call Flows (/investigate & /risk)
       ↓
Step 4: Ask AI CTO Advisor Grounded Questions (/ai-cto)
       ↓
Step 5: Simulate Architectural Changes (/simulate)
       ↓
Step 6: Authorize & Validate Autonomous Fixes (/improve)
```

---

### Step 1: Connect Your First Repository
1. Navigate to **Repositories & Software** (`⌘2` or `/repositories`).
2. Click **Connect Repository** and select your GitHub or GitLab repository.
3. CodeAtlas will begin progressive multi-stage analysis:
   - File & Symbol Parsing
   - Dependency Graph Extraction
   - Cross-Repository Coupling Analysis
   - Risk & Vulnerability Scanning

---

### Step 2: Explore Architecture & Knowledge Graph
1. Navigate to **Architecture Intelligence** (`⌘4` or `/architecture`).
2. Inspect the automatically rendered architectural diagram displaying service boundaries, API ingress points, and database connections.
3. Use `Contextual Graph Drawer` to trace symbol-level dependency links.

---

### Step 3: Investigate Engineering Risks & Call Flows
1. Navigate to **Cross-Org Risk Radar** (`⌘R` or `/risk`).
2. Select any active risk item (e.g. *CVE-2026-4491 in @acme/sec-vault*).
3. Click 1-click **Investigate** to inspect full execution call paths in `/investigate`.

---

### Step 4: Ask AI CTO Advisor Grounded Questions
1. Navigate to **AI CTO Advisor** (`⌘AI` or `/ai-cto`).
2. Ask evidence-backed queries such as:
   - *"Explain this repository architecture."*
   - *"What are the biggest architectural risks in payment-service?"*
   - *"How can we reduce coupling between checkout and analytics?"*

---

### Step 5: Simulate Changes & Authorize Improvements
1. Navigate to **Simulation Studio** (`⌘6` or `/simulate`) to evaluate hypothetical change scenarios without touching production.
2. Navigate to **Autonomous Optimization** (`⌘7` or `/improve`) to review code diff previews and authorize fixes via the Human Approval Gateway.

---

## Submitting Beta Feedback
Click **Give Beta Feedback** in the bottom left of the navigation sidebar at any time to submit feedback or bug reports. Contextual route details are automatically attached to help our team improve the product!
