## 3. Explicitly Deferred / Rejected Ideas

> [!WARNING]
> **REJECTED / DEFERRED IDEAS**
> - **Unvalidated AI Autonomous Mode without Human Approval**: REJECTED. Safety telemetry proves developers demand Level 4 Human Authorization.
> - **Decorative Dashboard Widgets**: REJECTED. Users interact strictly with the continuous 12-hub workspace workflow.
> - **Multi-Language Parsing Beyond Core 5**: DEFERRED to v1.2 until monorepo performance is further optimized.

## 4. v1.1 Release Criteria & Success Metrics

- **Monorepo Analysis Duration**: Reduce Tier 3 monorepo parsing time from 104s to **< 60s**.
- **Time to First Insight**: Maintain average time-to-first-insight **< 2 minutes**.
- **AI CTO Grounding**: Preserve **100% Grounding (0 Hallucinations)**.
- **Weekly Returning Developers**: Target **> 80% Retention**.
