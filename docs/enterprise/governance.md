# CodeAtlas Policy-as-Code & Governance Specification

## Policy Engine Outcomes

- `PASS`: All policy assertions satisfied.
- `WARN`: Non-blocking policy recommendation generated.
- `FAIL`: Policy violation detected; requires remediation or approved policy exception.
- `UNKNOWN`: Insufficient evidence to evaluate policy rule.

## Managed Exceptions Workflow

Policy exceptions require explicit owner assignment, executive approval, clear justification, defined expiration dates, and bounded organizational scope.
