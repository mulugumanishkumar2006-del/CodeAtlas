# Evidence-Based Engineering Health Model

## 7 Health Dimensions

1. **Architecture Boundary Integrity**: Cross-layer violations and drift metrics.
2. **Dependency Coupling**: Cross-repository coupling and call centrality.
3. **Change Churn Risk**: Deployment risk and scope adherence.
4. **Technical Debt Concentration**: AST complexity ($> 30$) and duplication trajectory.
5. **Security Signals**: Unredacted secrets and authorization path exposure.
6. **Service Reliability**: Readiness probes and health check status.
7. **Knowledge Availability**: ADR decision freshness and documentation grounding.
