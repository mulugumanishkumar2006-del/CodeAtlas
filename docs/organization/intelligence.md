# Organizational Intelligence — Overview & Architecture

## 1. Vision & Purpose

CodeAtlas Organizational Intelligence converts repository and graph evidence into executive-level and architect-level organizational clarity:

$$OBSERVE \rightarrow UNDERSTAND \rightarrow CONNECT \rightarrow DETECT \rightarrow PREDICT \rightarrow PRIORITIZE \rightarrow SIMULATE \rightarrow DECIDE \rightarrow ACT \rightarrow MEASURE \rightarrow LEARN$$

---

## 2. Core Pillars

1. **Organization Snapshots**: Consolidated snapshots across all microservices, teams, dependencies, and active migrations.
2. **7-Dimension Engineering Health**: Evidence-based health scores (Architecture, Dependency, Change Risk, Tech Debt, Security, Reliability, Knowledge).
3. **Single Points of Failure & Risk Concentration**: Technical SPOFs vs Ownership signals.
4. **$2\times2$ Priority Matrix**: $High\ Impact / High\ Risk$, $High\ Impact / Low\ Risk$, $Low\ Impact / High\ Risk$, $Low\ Impact / Low\ Risk$.
5. **Organizational AI Architect Assistant**: Evidence-grounded RAG assistant with citations.
